# Audio provenance

The prototype contains exactly six runtime sound-effect files. All are mono, 48 kHz, 16-bit WAV derivatives of CC0 sources.

| Runtime file | Source | License | Modifications | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `rifle_shot.wav` | [Gunshot Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds), `sks.wav`, collection by Tabasco; recording metadata credits Vince Sevedge | CC0 | Selected one SKS report; trimmed, mono-converted, filtered, faded, and gain-balanced | 2026-08-14 |
| `projectile_impact.wav` | [Kenney Impact Sounds](https://www.kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), `impactPunch_heavy_000.ogg` and `impactSoft_heavy_002.ogg` | CC0 | Layered into one short flesh-impact cue; filtered, trimmed, faded, and gain-balanced | 2026-08-14 |
| `monster_attack.wav` | [Kenney RPG Audio](https://www.kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio), `knifeSlice.ogg` and `creak2.ogg` | CC0 | Pitch-shifted and layered into one inhuman swing/groan cue; filtered, trimmed, and faded | 2026-08-14 |
| `player_damage.wav` | [Kenney Impact Sounds](https://www.kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), `impactPunch_medium_002.ogg` | CC0 | Filtered, trimmed, faded, mono-converted, and gain-balanced | 2026-08-14 |
| `monster_death.wav` | Kenney Impact Sounds `impactSoft_heavy_003.ogg`; Kenney RPG Audio `creak1.ogg` | CC0 | Pitch-shifted and layered into one heavy collapse/groan; filtered, faded, and reduced 3 dB for safe peak headroom | 2026-08-14 |
| `player_death.wav` | Kenney RPG Audio `dropLeather.ogg` and `metalClick.ogg`; Kenney Impact Sounds `impactSoft_heavy_001.ogg` | CC0 | Layered into one body-fall/equipment-rattle cue; delayed, filtered, trimmed, faded, and gain-balanced | 2026-08-14 |

Attribution is not legally required by CC0, but source credits are retained for traceability and team handoff.
